How it works

Three steps. Under a minute.

The math

100% of what a play earns is yours.

100% → YOUFEE

No mystery yield, no deposits, no "stake to earn." Encore earns from affiliation and partner revenue on the music you play — routed to the listener as stock tokens instead of kept.

Encore takes no cut of your plays. Withdrawals carry only a small network fee to cover the on-chain transfer — shown upfront, deducted from the payout, never a surprise.

Why it's safe

One reward. Per listener. Per play.

Crypto is full of apps that ask you to connect a wallet and then drain it. Encore is non-custodial — it never touches your keys or your funds. Rewards settle to your wallet against a public, auditable record.

Rewards are real value, so a fake listen is theft. The play-verification logic deduplicates by listener and play, blocks bot floods, and won't pay the same track twice — and it's all open source, so you don't have to trust us on it.
