How it works

Three steps. Under a minute.

You're not signing up for a rewards scheme. You press play on music you'd listen to anyway, and a slice of what that play earns comes back to you.

01
Play

Press play on a partnered track

Artists and labels opt their releases into Encore. Only affiliated plays earn — no gimmicks, no staking.

02
Verify

The listen is checked, then attributed

One real listen, one reward. Bots, double-plays and the same track twice don't count — the first genuine play is the one that pays.

03
Own

SOL lands in your wallet

Paid in SOL to a wallet you control. Encore never holds your keys or your funds.

The math

100% of what a play earns is yours.

100% → YOUFEE

No mystery yield, no deposits, no "stake to earn." Encore earns from affiliation and partner revenue on the music you play — the same kind of payout a playlist or a promoter earns, routed to the listener instead.

Encore takes no cut of your plays. The platform's only fee is a flat withdrawal fee, shown transparently when you cash out and deducted from the payout — never charged before, and never a surprise.

Why it's safe

One reward. Per listener. Per play.

Crypto is full of apps that ask you to connect a wallet and then drain it. Encore is non-custodial — it never touches your keys or your funds. Payouts settle to your wallet against a public, auditable reward record.

Rewards are real value, so a fake listen is theft. The play-verification logic deduplicates by listener and play, blocks bot floods, and won't pay the same track twice — and it's all open source, so you don't have to trust us on it.

Open source

Don't trust us. Read it.

The reward-attribution core is public. Clone it, run the tests, or just search it for the things a drainer would need — you won't find them.
