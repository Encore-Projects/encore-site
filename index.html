How it works

Three steps. Under a minute.

You're not signing up for a rewards scheme. You press play on music you'd listen to anyway, and a slice of a stock token comes back to you.

01

Each track is tied to a stock token — AAPL, NVDA, META and more. Only affiliated plays earn.

02
Verify

One real listen, one reward

Bots, double-plays and the same track twice don't count — the first genuine play is the one that pays.

Stock tokens land in your wallet

Sent to a wallet you control, on Robinhood Chain. You receive tokens, you buy nothing, and Encore never holds your funds.

The math

100% of what a play earns is yours.

100% → YOUFEE

No mystery yield, no deposits, no "stake to earn." Encore earns from affiliation and partner revenue on the music you play — routed to the listener as stock tokens instead of kept.

Encore takes no cut of your plays. The platform's only fee is a flat withdrawal fee, shown transparently when you cash out and deducted from the payout — never charged before, and never a surprise.

Why it's safe

One reward. Per listener. Per play.

Crypto is full of apps that ask you to connect a wallet and then drain it. Encore is non-custodial — it never touches your keys or your funds. Rewards settle to your wallet against a public, auditable record.

Rewards are real value, so a fake listen is theft. The play-verification logic deduplicates by listener and play, blocks bot floods, and won't pay the same track twice — and it's all open source, so you don't have to trust us on it.

Open source

Don't trust us. Read it.
