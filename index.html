A2

Where the money comes from

No mystery yield, no deposits, no "stake to earn." Encore earns from affiliation and partner revenue on the music you play — the same kind of payout a playlist or a promoter earns, routed to the listener instead.

100% → YOU FEE

At launch, 100% of the revenue a play generates goes to the listener. Encore keeps nothing for now — the goal is to grow the listening base first. A small platform fee may be introduced later, and it'll be shown here in the open when it is.

A3

Open source, on purpose

Crypto is full of apps that ask you to connect a wallet and then drain it. Encore is non-custodial — it never holds your keys or your funds — and the play-verification and reward logic is public, so you don't have to take our word for it.
